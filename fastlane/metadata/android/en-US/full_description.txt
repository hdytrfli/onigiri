Findroid+ is a native Android client for Jellyfin. Browse and play movies, shows, books, and audiobooks from your own Jellyfin server.

The app includes a built-in EPUB, PDF, and CBZ reader, per-profile PIN protection, downloads for offline use, and both ExoPlayer and mpv playback.

Findroid+ is an independent fork of the Findroid project. It is not affiliated with Jellyfin or the Findroid project.
